# Fidelity review brief

Compare every clause in the draft with the supplied Latin. For each finding
give the reference, Latin words, English words, severity, explanation, and
proposed remedy. Check negation, syntax, scope, lists, omissions, additions,
quotations, technical terms, and etymological wordplay. Record uncertain
readings separately. Do not praise or accept the book as a substitute for
an explicit findings record. A review is not evidence of source collation.
