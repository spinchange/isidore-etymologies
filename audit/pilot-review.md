## Notes and limits

Notes explain wordplay, significant brackets, and the difference between the
text's assertion and a modern comparison; they do not merely repeat paragraphs.
Modern biology note checked against University of Florida/IFAS IN1005:
https://ask.ifas.ufl.edu/publication/IN1005 (accessed 2026-11-01).
It supports the egg/larva/pupa/adult comparison, not the ancient derivations.

No modern English translation was used as a drafting base or wording source.
Aristotle/Cicero/Varro source identifications and a complete historical source
apparatus remain future editorial work. These open points do not stop a clearly
labeled voice/design pilot; they must not be reported as completed scholarship.

The edition is abridged by selection, not by silently compressing paragraphs.
Task hashes and alignment checks certify structure and preserved inputs, not
the truth of Isidore's claims or the literary quality of the translation.
