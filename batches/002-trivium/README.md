# Batch 002: grammar, rhetoric, dialectic

Sixteen complete numbered sections in seven selections, added after the editor
approved the initial pilot and requested a prominent trivium focus. This batch
does not replace or silently revise the approved pilot.

`source.json` is the fixed, collated input. `source-corrections.json` records
print readings and the split of II.1.2, which the HTML merges into II.1.1.
Collated against volume I PDF pages 95-96, 113-114, plus the alternate
volume-I copy's page 102 for I.41. The first consultation scan jumps from
I.39 to I.42 between PDF pages 93 and 94; the alternate scan supplies I.41.

Full chapters: I.41, II.1, II.2, II.3, II.22. Excerpts with intact sections:
II.4.1-4 and II.23.1-2. The further examples in II.4 and the transition to
philosophy in II.23.3 remain outside this batch, not silently compressed.

Two low-reasoning tasks draft the fixed ten-section oratory group and the
six-section logic/history group. Their outputs remain proposals. The lead
reads every selected section against the printed Latin, records decisions,
and assembles the reviewed batch into the cumulative reader. Task records
preserve model configuration, dispatched instruction files, refs, and hashes.
