# Rights and provenance

Original project contributions, including the translation, notes, introduction,
indexes, original art, and code, are dedicated under CC0 1.0. Contributors must
have the right to make that dedication. CC0 does not alter third-party rights.
The official instrument is included in LICENSE:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode.en

Lindsay's 1911 Latin edition is the intended source. Internet Archive marks
the verified volume-I copy Public Domain. A modern site's transcription,
added notes, design, or corrections should not be assumed to share that label.
Record each actual import and its terms in latin/SOURCE.md before release.

Do not import wording from modern English translations. They may be used for
comparison only with appropriate access and citations, not as draft material.
Do not include fonts, images, or other third-party assets without recording
their source and terms here. Font licenses must travel with embedded fonts
where required; a CC0 project cannot waive those terms.

No fonts, images, scans, or third-party transcriptions are bundled yet. The
LICENSE file is the official CC0 legal instrument, reproduced as such.

Selling printed copies does not restrict anyone's right to download, reprint,
adapt, or sell the CC0 work. Preserve that explanation in the eventual book
and website. Credit is requested as a scholarly courtesy, never required by CC0.
